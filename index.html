XCO Speed Test measures your download, upload, ping and jitter using Cloudflare's public speed servers. Results are estimates and vary with network conditions, Wi-Fi quality and other devices on your network.

About XCO Speed Test

XCO is a free, ad-free internet speed test. It checks your download speed, upload speed, latency (ping) and jitter in about 20 seconds, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

FAQ

What does XCO Speed Test measure?

Download and upload speed in Mbps, latency (ping) in milliseconds, and jitter, which is how much your latency varies. Tests run against Cloudflare's public speed servers.

What is a good ping and jitter?

Under 30 ms ping and under 5 ms jitter feels great for gaming and video calls. Under 100 ms is fine for general browsing.

Why does my speed differ from my plan?

Wi-Fi quality, other devices, distance to the test server and congestion all affect results. For the most accurate reading, use a wired connection and pause other downloads.
