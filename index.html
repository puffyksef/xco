About XCO Speed Test

XCO is a free, ad-free internet speed test. In about 25 seconds it measures download and upload speed, ping, jitter and latency under load (bufferbloat), then tells you what your connection can handle. No sign-up, nothing to install, and your history stays on your device.

FAQ

What is bufferbloat?

The extra delay your connection adds when it is busy. High bufferbloat causes lag in games and calls while someone downloads or streams.

What is a good ping and jitter?

Under 30 ms ping and under 5 ms jitter is excellent for gaming and calls. Under 100 ms is fine for browsing.

Why is my speed lower than my plan?

Wi-Fi quality, other devices, server distance and congestion all matter. Use a wired connection and pause other downloads for the most accurate result.
